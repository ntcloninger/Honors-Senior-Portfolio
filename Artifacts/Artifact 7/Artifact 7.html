Metamorphoses Play

This artifact is about the fact that I am actively participating in an acting role in the NC State University Theaters production of Ovid's Metamophoses adapted by Mary Zimmerman. Now since of writing this artifact the produciton is still ongoing I cannot give any photos or too many details from behind the scenes. I will say though that this play has challenged me to focus on both myselfs, my personal relationships, and the other members of the cast as I would not want to disapoint them. This play has shown me how much I love acting as being a part of a crew. It has pushed my to further organize my time to both attend to my physical and mental health but also to my academics as well. I do hope to in the future continue down this avenue in some form or another.
